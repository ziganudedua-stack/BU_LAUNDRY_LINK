
LaundryLink — Product Requirement Document

##1.	Problem Overview

Students at Babcock University regularly struggle to access convenient laundry services. Most students live in hostels with limited washing facilities, tight academic schedules, and restricted movement around campus. When clothes pile up, students often have to carry heavy bags to laundry operators, wait for availability, or rely on word-of-mouth recommendations to find someone who can wash their clothes.

The process is disorganised. Students do not know which laundry services are available, how much they charge, how long they will take, or whether their clothes are ready. Laundry 

operators, on the other hand, struggle to find customers consistently and manage orders without a structured system.

Our team wants to investigate these challenges and determine whether a digital platform could connect students with laundry service providers on and around Babcock University campus.

##2.Research Objectives

The purpose of this research is to:

· Understand how students currently handle their laundry.
· Identify the major laundry problems students experience.
· Understand the challenges faced by laundry service providers.

· Find out how students currently find and communicate with laundry operators.
· Determine whether students would use a digital laundry booking platform.
· Identify the features users would consider most useful.

##3.	Research Method

Our team will conduct research using:

· Student Survey.
· Direct observation around campus hostels and laundry points.

The findings will be used to determine the actual problem and define the requirements for the proposed solution.

5.2 Main Problem Identified

The preliminary observation suggests that access to organised laundry services is a significant challenge for students. Students currently depend mainly on physically finding laundry operators, carrying clothes themselves, and following up manually. This can make it difficult to know availability, pricing, and readiness of clothes.

The findings will support the idea of a platform such as LaundryLink that allows students to book laundry services digitally.

##6.	Target Users


6.1 Students

The primary users of LaundryLink are the students who need laundry services. They may use it to:

· Book a laundry service.
· Enter their pickup location and preferred time.
· View available laundry providers and prices.
· Receive order confirmation and status updates.
· Track the status of their laundry.
· Pay for services.

6.2 Laundry Service Providers

They are the secondary users of LaundryLink. They may use it to:

· Set their availability.

· Receive laundry requests.
· View pickup and delivery information.
· Accept or reject requests.
· Update order status.
· Mark orders as completed.

6.3 Administrators

They will manage the platform and help ensure that students and laundry providers use the system appropriately. Admins may:

· Manage users.
· Verify laundry providers.
· Monitor orders.
· Handle reports and complaints.

##7.	Proposed Solution

LaundryLink (Babcock Laundry Booking Platform) is a proposed digital platform that connects students with available laundry service providers around Babcock University.

Instead of students having to physically search for a laundry operator, they can use LaundryLink to submit a laundry request.

The basic process will be:

Student books a laundry service ↓ Available laundry providers receive the request ↓ Provider accepts the request ↓ Student receives provider information and pickup details ↓ Provider picks up clothes ↓ Laundry is processed ↓ Provider delivers clothes ↓ Order is completed

The solution is intended to make the laundry process more organised, convenient, and easier 

to access.

##9.	Functional & Non-Functional Requirements

9.1 Functional Requirements

The system should:

· Allow students and providers to create accounts and log in.
· Allow students to book laundry services.
· Allow students to enter pickup and preferred time.
· Allow available providers to receive order requests.
· Allow providers to accept or reject requests.
· Display relevant provider information to students.
· Allow users to view order status.
· Allow providers to mark orders as completed.

· Allow students to cancel order requests.
· Allow administrators to manage users and providers.

9.2 Non-Functional Requirements

The system should be:

· Easy to use: Users should be able to understand the main functions without extensive instructions.
· Fast: Order requests and important actions should receive a quick response.
· Secure: User accounts and personal information should be protected.
· Reliable: Order information should not be unnecessarily lost or corrupted.
· Scalable: The platform should be capable of supporting more users and laundry services in the future.


##10.	Risks and Assumptions

10.1 Risks

| Risk                          | Impact                                    | Mitigation |
|---                            |---                                        |---|
| Few providers use LaundryLink | Students may not find available services  | Recruit more providers |
| Poor internet connection      | Requests may fail or be delayed           | Keep the platform lightweight |
| Unverified providers          | Safety and quality concerns               | Verify providers |
| Low student adoption          | Few students may use the platform         | Test with students |

10.2 Assumptions

The project assumes that:

· Students have access to smartphones.
· Students have internet access.
· Laundry providers are willing to use the platform.
· Providers can be properly verified.
· Campus pickup locations can be identified.
· There is sufficient demand for laundry services.
· The proposed service can operate within applicable university rules.
